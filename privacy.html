Privacy Policy: Nickel and Why Tools

Last updated: 7 October 2026

"Nickel and Why Tools" (the "app") is a private tool used only by the team behind the Nickel & Why YouTube channel to manage that one channel. It is not offered to anyone else.

What the app accesses

When the channel owner signs in with Google, the app asks for permission to:

  • view and manage the channel's YouTube videos, playlists and captions (to upload videos, schedule them and edit titles, descriptions and thumbnails);
  • view the channel's YouTube Analytics reports (views, watch time and similar numbers).

How the data is used and stored

  • Data is used only to manage and measure the Nickel & Why channel.
  • The sign-in token and any downloaded reports are stored only on the channel owner's own computer. The app has no server and no database.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.
  • No data about viewers or other Google users is collected.

Google API Services

The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. The app uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the local token file removes all stored data.
